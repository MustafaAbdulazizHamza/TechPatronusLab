# Artificial Intelligence in Cyber Defense: Applications and Risks
> Presented at NTU — 4 March 2026.
=== "Abstract"
    This presentation outlines the importance and applications of Artificial Intelligence in cyber defense, highlighting three key examples. Additionally, it examines the concept of adversarial AI and demonstrates a simple example of a 'Good Words' attack.
